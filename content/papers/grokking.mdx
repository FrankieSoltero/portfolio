Train a small transformer on a simple algorithmic task like modular addition. It
memorizes the training set quickly — then, thousands of steps *after* training
accuracy hits 100%, validation accuracy suddenly snaps from chance to near-perfect.
The model "groks" the underlying rule long after it appeared to be done learning.

$$
\text{val acc} \approx \frac{1}{p} \;\; \xrightarrow{\;\sim 10^4 \text{ steps later}\;} \;\; \approx 1.0
$$

## Why it matters

It's a clean, cheap window into generalization dynamics — evidence that
"overfitting" isn't the end of the story, and that weight decay and training
duration interact in ways the classic bias-variance picture doesn't predict.
